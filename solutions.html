Solutions

Connectivity and power infrastructure, engineered end-to-end.

Survey, design, installation, commissioning, and certification — delivered by one accountable team that knows how the systems fit together.

Distributed Antenna Systems

Cellular coverage that works in every corner.

Today everyone depends on their mobile device, and dead zones cost productivity and revenue. We design and deploy in-building DAS for office towers, hospitals, airports, stadiums, and large outdoor venues — so service is reliable wherever your people are.

  • DAS surveys, iBwave design & link budgets
  • Carrier coordination and approvals
  • Implementation, commissioning & benchmark testing
Public Safety DAS

When seconds matter, the signal has to hold.

A Public Safety DAS (ERRCS) ensures firefighters and police can communicate indoors during an emergency. We design and install code-compliant systems that keep first-responder networks alive throughout your building — and get them approved by the authority having jurisdiction.

  • NFPA 72 & IFC code-compliant design
  • AHJ coordination and acceptance testing
  • Annual testing & maintenance support
Wireless, WiFi & Small Cell

Capacity, coverage, and control.

Superior WiFi is critical for voice, data, and location services. We deliver WiFi design, interference tracking, and optimization, plus private LTE/5G (CBRS) and small cell deployment to expand capacity in dense, high-traffic environments.

  • Active & passive WiFi surveys
  • Private LTE / CBRS networks
  • Small cell siting & deployment
Fiber & Structured Cabling

The backbone everything else runs on.

Almost every technology install starts with quality cabling. We provide high-capacity fiber backbone and structured low-voltage cabling for voice, data, and video — built to standard and documented for the long haul.

  • Single-mode & multimode fiber
  • Structured cabling for data centers & offices
  • Certified testing & closeout documentation
Electrical & EV Charging

  • Commercial electrical design & install
  • EV charging station deployment
  • Code-compliant, permitted & inspected
