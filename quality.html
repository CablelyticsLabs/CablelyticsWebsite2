The 360 Process

Six stages. One accountable circle.

Every Cablelytics 360 project follows the same disciplined loop, so nothing gets missed and you always know exactly where things stand.

1

Survey & Assessment

RF and site surveys (active and passive), data collection, and benchmark testing establish exactly what your building needs before a single cable is pulled.

2

Engineering & Design

iBwave design, link budgets, and code review produce a documented plan engineered for performance and compliance.

3

Carrier & AHJ Coordination

We manage carrier approvals and authority-having-jurisdiction coordination so the paperwork never stalls your timeline.

4

Safe, Quality Implementation

Licensed, insured technicians install to standard with safety as the priority — telecom and electrical handled by one accountable team.

5

Commissioning & Testing

CW testing, benchmark verification, and acceptance testing confirm the system performs exactly as designed.

6

Closeout & Ongoing Support

Detailed closeout packages document everything, and ongoing testing & maintenance keep the system compliant for years — closing the loop.
